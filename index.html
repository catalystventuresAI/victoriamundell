Victoria Mundell

Interior Design Student · Coquitlam, BC

As an interior design student, I have a heightened appreciation for how space shapes experience. My sensitivity to emotions informs a human-centered design approach that I take into each project. I strive to create spaces that support rest, connection, and healthy living, drawing inspiration from nature, culture, and wellness practices.

When I'm not designing, I'm an avid world traveller, gaining new perspectives and developing skills in a breadth of industries.

Career Objective

After graduation, I hope to begin my career as a junior interior designer within the hospitality and retail industries. I am eager to work closely with clients and users on both domestic and international projects, creating spaces that are thoughtful, functional and memorable.
