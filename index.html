Victoria Mundell

Interior Design Student · Coquitlam, BC

As an interior design student, I have a heightened appreciation for how space shapes experience. My sensitivity to emotions informs a human-centered design approach that I take into each project. I strive to create spaces that support rest, connection, and well-being, drawing inspiration from cultural context and hospitality design principles.

When I'm not designing, I'm an avid world traveller, exploring new cultures and developing skills in a breadth of industries.
